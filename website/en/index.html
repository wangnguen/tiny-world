What do pets do all day?

No stats, no quests. Just a few little friends living next to the windows you have open.

  • Climbing your windows

    Pets climb up the side of a window, stroll along the top, sit dangling off a corner, then hop over to another window. Drag the window and they ride along; minimize it and they fall.

  • Click, drag, fling

    Click and a pet hops, drag it up and let go and it drops, fling the mouse and it flies with the momentum. Drop it from too high and it gets dizzy, with stars spinning around its head.

  • You're busy, they nap

    After three minutes without attention, the whole group heads down to the taskbar and naps side by side. Click anywhere on the screen to wake them.

  • Real weather around them

    Pick your city: rain, snow, sun, thunder or fog shows up right around your pets. Heat haze rises when it's hot, and they breathe little clouds when it's cold.

  • Double-click for the time

    A pet tells you the time, the day, the date, the lunar date and the weather in your city.

  • Gentle health reminders

    Sat too long? A pet reminds you to rest your eyes, drink some water, and go to bed when it's late. Off by default, turn on what you need.

  • Quick questions, short answers

    Right-click a pet to ask about exchange rates, gold prices, unit conversions or get help writing a wish, without opening a browser. No account needed.

  • Remembers special days

    Lunar New Year, Mid-Autumn, Christmas... pets say a wish on the day. Add your own birthdays and anniversaries, on the solar or lunar calendar.

  • Up to three at once

    When they meet they stop to say hi, sometimes walk together for a bit, and at bedtime they sleep side by side.

Light and never in the way

  • No interruptions

    No sounds, no popups. Watch a video or play a game in full screen and the pets hide themselves, then come back when you're done.

  • Never steals your clicks

    Your mouse passes through every empty spot. Clicking a pet doesn't take focus away from what you're typing.

  • Light on your PC

    When the group is asleep the app practically rests. Pets only redraw when something changes.

  • Private

    Never reads window titles or contents, never records your keystrokes. Screen time data stays on your computer.

  • English and Vietnamese

    The app follows your Windows language, and you can switch anytime in Settings.

  • Updates itself

    When a new version is out a pet tells you, and one click installs it.
