Original pixel-art sprite sheet for Quác, a NEW original character in my own desktop-pet game. Image 1 is a STYLE REFERENCE ONLY: it shows a different character from the same game. Match its art style exactly (cute compact chibi proportions with a big head, crisp flat pixel-art clusters, clean dark outline, flat opaque colours, the same pixel density and the same character size inside each cell), but do NOT copy its species, colours, face, clothing, accessories or markings. Draw only the new character described below.
Character: Quác, a chaotic chibi white goose clown with meme energy. Design: plump white goose with a short chibi neck, a big orange beak that is often wide open mid-HONK showing a pink tongue, one wild wide eye with a tiny pupil, a frilly clown ruff collar around the neck in red, yellow and blue segments, and a tiny red cone party hat with a white pom-pom tilted on its head.
Anatomy, required in EVERY cell: TWO white wings used as arms at the shoulders and TWO orange legs with orange webbed feet, plus a short white tail. Every limb is attached to the body in the anatomically correct place, has normal length, and is clearly visible with its own outline and fill colour (an occluded far limb is drawn slightly darker but still visible). Never drop, merge, duplicate or float a limb; no extra limbs. right-facing side view with ONE visible wild eye.
Layout: ONE image, 4:3 wide, 4 columns x 3 rows = 12 equal square cells, real transparent background (alpha 0). Exactly one full-body character per cell, centred in the cell, the WHOLE body (ears, hat, tail, feet) inside the cell with a clear transparent margin; nothing may touch or cross a cell border and nothing may be cropped. Read rows top to bottom, frames left to right. Face RIGHT in every frame. Same character size in every row (head the same size as in row 1). No grid lines, no labels, no text, no numbers, no ground, no wall, no shadow, no speech marks, no stars, no motion lines, no effects.
Row 1: IDLE (looping): standing calmly on the ground, gentle breathing; frame 3 blinks (eyes closed), frames 1, 2 and 4 eyes open.
Row 2: WALK (looping 4-phase gait, moving right): 1 near (lighter) foot planted forward to the RIGHT, far (darker) foot behind to the LEFT; 2 near foot supporting under the hip, far foot bent and swinging low under the hip; 3 far foot planted forward to the RIGHT, near foot behind to the LEFT, the reverse of frame 1; 4 far foot supporting under the hip, near heel lifted back, the reverse of frame 2. Arms swing opposite to the legs while staying attached at the shoulders. The two feet MUST switch front/back position between frames 1 and 3.
Row 3: RUN (looping 4-phase gait, moving right): same alternating leg ownership as walk with a wider stride and bent-knee recovery, gentle forward lean, feet a little off the ground on the passing frames; legs keep their normal short length, no high kicks.
Style: crisp flat pixel-art clusters with a clean dark outline exactly like Image 1, 12-18 flat opaque colours, no gradients, no blur, no soft antialias haze, no grain or noise, no checkerboard.
Character notes: Quác is a goose: wherever a row mentions arms or hands, use its two white wings; its feet are orange webbed feet. Expressive meme-style face: the beak is wide open in a loud HONK in idle frame 2, react, fall and jump, otherwise a smug half-open beak. The clown ruff and the tilted party hat stay on in every frame.
